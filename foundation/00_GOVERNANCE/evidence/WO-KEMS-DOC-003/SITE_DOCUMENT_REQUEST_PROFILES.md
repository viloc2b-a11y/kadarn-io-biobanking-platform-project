## Profile-Specific Notes

### CRS (Clinical Research Site)
Standard profile; requires full regulatory package. All PERSON_REUSABLE types required for all credentialed staff. Study-specific documents (1572, Delegation Log, Study-Specific Training) required per study. Insurance Certificate and Indemnification/Liability Coverage required.

### Community
Similar to CRS but conditional on site capabilities. May not have CLIA, CAP, or advanced equipment. Higher reliance on structured data for recruitment/diversity evidence. Community Advisory Board Documentation and Translator/Language Services are required.

### Phase I
Requires Medical License, ACLS/BLS, Pharmacy License, Controlled-Substance Registration. Equipment calibration/qualification is conditional (may outsource lab). Emergency Response Plan and Business Continuity Plan are required. Has dedicated overnight observation capability.

### Central Lab
All EQUIPMENT_REUSABLE and LOCATION_REUSABLE types required. CLIA/CAP are required. Personnel certifications required for all testing staff. MTA is conditional (depends on sample receipt/shipping). CAPA Records and Internal Audit Reports are restricted (internal quality).

### Biorepo
All EQUIPMENT_REUSABLE types required (cold chain monitoring, temperature mapping). MTA is required for all collections. Personnel certifications are required. Imaging and recruitment profiles are N/A. Shipping Equipment Validation is required.

### Coll Network
Balanced profile. Personnel credentials required for all staff across sites. IRB Reliance Information is required for multi-site IRB model. Equipment evidence is conditional per site. Financial Disclosure required for all investigators.

### Dx/IVD
CLIA, CAP accreditation are required. Equipment calibration, qualification, and maintenance are required. 21 CFR Part 11 is required for electronic records. FDA 1572 and Medical License are conditional (investigational vs. standard-of-care). Data Security Certification is required.

### Imaging
Radiation Safety Certificate is required. Medical License and Board Certification are required for interpreting physicians. Equipment calibration/qualification is required for imaging modalities. CLIA is N/A. BSL Documentation is N/A.

### Specialty
Similar to CRS but Medical License and Board Certification are required. Equipment evidence is conditional based on on-site capabilities. Patient recruitment and diversity plans are recommended. Protocol-specific training emphasized.

### Processing
CLIA is required. All EQUIPMENT_REUSABLE types required for processing equipment. Personnel credentials conditional on test complexity (CLIA-waived vs. high complexity). Recruitment profiles are N/A. Hazardous Materials Documentation required.

### Recruit Network
Patient Recruitment Plan and Diversity and Inclusion Plan are required. Community Advisory Board Documentation is required. Translator/Language Services is required. Personnel credentials required for all outreach staff. Lab/equipment profiles are N/A. IRB Reliance Information is required.

### Data/Tech
Data Security Certification is required. EMR/EHR System Validation is required. 21 CFR Part 11 Compliance is conditional (depends on regulated data). All EQUIPMENT_REUSABLE types apply if hosting regulated infrastructure. Clinical personnel credentials are N/A. Staff Training Compliance Matrix is required.
